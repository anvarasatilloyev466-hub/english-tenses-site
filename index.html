English 12 Tenses / Ingliz tili 12 zamoni / Забон англисӣ 12 замон

Present Simple / Hozirgi oddiy zamon / Замони ҳозираи оддӣ

Structure: Subject + base verb (V1 / -s/-es 3rd person singular)

Used: Habits, general truths, routines

Example:
English: I play football every day.
Uzbek: Men har kuni futbol o‘ynayman.
Tajik: Ман ҳар рӯз футбол бозӣ мекунам.

Difference: Habitual actions vs Present Continuous (ongoing actions)

Additional info: 3rd person singular adds -s/-es; Question: Do/Does?; Negative: do/does not

Present Continuous / Hozirgi davomli zamon/ Замони ҳозираи давомдор

Structure: Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

Used: Actions happening now, near-future plans

Example:
English: She is cooking dinner now.
Uzbek: U hozir kechki ovqat pishiryapti.
Tajik: Вай ҳозир шомро омода мекунад.

Difference: Present Simple is for habits; Present Continuous is for actions happening now

Additional info: Question: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?; Negative: am/is/are + not + verb-ing

Present Perfect / Hozirgi mukammal zamon/ Замони ҳозираи мукаммал

Structure: Subject + have/has + past participle (V3)

Used: Actions completed at present, experiences, results

Example:
English: They have watched that movie.
Uzbek: Ular o‘sha filmni ko‘rishgan.
Tajik: Онҳо он филмро тамошо кардаанд.

Difference: Past Simple vs Present Perfect: Present Perfect connects past with present result

Additional info: Question: Have/Has + subject + V3?; Negative: have/has + not + V3

Present Perfect Continuous / Hozirgi davomli mukammal zamon/ Замони ҳозираи мукаммали давомдор

Structure: Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing

Used: Actions that started in the past and continue until now

Example:
English: I have been studying for two hours.
Uzbek: Men ikki soatdirki o‘qiyapman.
Tajik: Ман ду соат боз меомӯзам.

Difference: Emphasizes duration of action

Additional info: Question: Have/Has + subject + been + verb-ing?; Negative: have/has + not + been + verb-ing

Past Simple / O‘tgan oddiy zamon / Замони гузаштаи оддӣ

Structure: Subject + past verb (V2)

Used: Completed actions at a definite past time

Example:
English: She went to the market yesterday.
Uzbek: U kecha bozorga bordi.
Tajik: Вай дирӯз ба бозор рафт.

Difference: Specifies exact past time, unlike Present Perfect

Additional info: Question: Did + subject + base verb?; Negative: did + not + base verb

Past Continuous / O‘tgan davomli zamon/ Замони гузаштаи давомдор

Structure: Subject + was/were + verb-ing

Used: Actions in progress at a specific past time

Example:
English: I was reading when she called.
Uzbek: U qo‘ng‘iroq qilganida men kitob o‘qiyapman edi.
Tajik: Ман ҳангоми зангашон хондан будам.

Difference: Past Simple is finished; Past Continuous emphasizes ongoing action

Additional info: Question: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?; Negative: was/were + not + verb-ing

Past Perfect / O‘tgan mukammal zamon/ Замони гузаштаи мукаммал

Structure: Subject + had + V3

Used: Action completed before another past action

Example:
English: I had finished homework before dinner.
Uzbek: Men kechki ovqatdan oldin uy vazifasini tugatgan edim.
Tajik: Ман пеш аз хӯроки шом корро анҷом дода будам.

Difference: Shows prior completion

Additional info: Question: Had + subject + V3?; Negative: had + not + V3

Past Perfect Continuous / O‘tgan davomli mukammal zamon / Замони гузаштаи мукаммали давомдор

Structure: Subject + had + been + verb-ing

Used: Actions continuing up to a point in the past

Example:
English: I had been working for 3 hours before he arrived.
Uzbek: U kelishidan oldin men 3 soat ishlayotgan edim.
Tajik: Ман пеш аз он ки ӯ расид, се соат кор мекардам.

Difference: Emphasizes duration before another past action

Additional info: Question: Had + subject + been + verb-ing?; Negative: had + not + been + verb-ing

Future Simple / Kelasi zamon / Замони оянда

Structure: Subject + will + base verb

Used: Predictions, spontaneous decisions, future facts

Example:
English: I will call you tomorrow.
Uzbek: Men ertaga sizga qo‘ng‘iroq qilaman.
Tajik: Ман фардо ба шумо занг мезанам.

Difference: Emphasizes spontaneous or general future

Additional info: Question: Will + subject + base verb?; Negative: will + not + base verb

Future Continuous / Kelasi davomli zamon/ Замони ояндаи давомдор

Structure: Subject + will be + verb-ing

Used: Actions in progress at a future time

Example:
English: I will be studying at 6 pm.
Uzbek: Men soat 6 da o‘qiyapman bo‘laman.
Tajik: Ман соати 6 хондан хоҳам буд.

Difference: Focus on ongoing action at a specific future time

Additional info: Question: Will + subject + be + verb-ing?; Negative: will + not + be + verb-ing

Future Perfect / Kelasi mukammal zamon / Замони ояндаи мукаммал

Structure: Subject + will have + V3

Used: Actions completed before a certain future time

Example:
English: I will have finished my homework by 8 pm.
Uzbek: Men soat 8 ga qadar uy vazifasini tugatgan bo‘laman.
Tajik: Ман то соати 8 кори хонагӣ анҷом дода хоҳам буд.

Difference: Emphasizes completion before a future point

Additional info: Question: Will + subject + have + V3?; Negative: will + not + have + V3

Future Perfect Continuous / Kelasi davomli mukammal zamon/ Замони ояндаи мукаммали давомдор

Structure: Subject + will + have + been + verb-ing

Used: Actions continuing up to a point in the future

Example:
English: By next month, I will have been working here for 2 years.
Uzbek: Keyingi oyga kelib, men bu yerda 2 yil ishlayotgan bo‘laman.
Tajik: То моҳи оянда ман ду сол инҷо кор хоҳам кард.

Difference: Focus on duration of future action before a certain time

Additional info: Question: Will + subject + have + been + verb-ing?; Negative: will + not + have + been + verb-ing
